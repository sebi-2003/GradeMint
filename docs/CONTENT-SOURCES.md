# Content and sources

Teacher facts supplied by the owner: Sebi offers mathematics tutoring and has done so for two years. Bianca offers Romanian tutoring. No academic qualifications, testimonials, success rates, examination grades, student counts, prices or availability have been invented.

Archive sources checked on 19 October 2026:

- https://subiectebac.ro/mate/matematica.html — index of mathematics years.
- https://subiectebac.ro/romana/romana.html — index of Romanian years.
- https://subiectebac.ro/mate/mate_2015.html and mate_2026.html — year archive format and subject/barem links.
- https://subiectebac.ro/romana/romana_2015.html and romana_2026.html — Romanian year archive format.
- https://subiecte.edu.ro/2026/bacalaureat/Subiecte_si_bareme/ — official Ministry portal (intermittent availability).

The catalog intentionally links to source pages rather than republishing third-party pages or asserting ownership over official exam PDFs. Users choose the specific profile/session and barem at the source. The app does not assert that every historical external file will remain available. Recheck links periodically.

All practice questions in `data/question-bank.js` and all promotional copy were authored for this project. Practice results are not official exam grades. Romanian essay writing and full BAC papers still require tutor evaluation beyond the initial multiple-choice bank.
